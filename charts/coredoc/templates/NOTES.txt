2. First workspace / admin bootstrap:

   A user who passes the login gate can sign in but has NO workspace access
   until they are a member. Seed the very first owner directly in Postgres
   (one time), keyed by the email matching their verified identity:

     INSERT INTO workspaces (name, slug) VALUES ('Acme', 'acme') RETURNING id;
     INSERT INTO workspace_members (workspace_id, user_id, email, role, pending)
     VALUES ('<workspace-id>', 'pending:admin@example.com', 'admin@example.com', 'owner', true);

   On that admin's first login the pending row is linked to their real user;
   they then invite everyone else via the members API.

3. Connect an MCP client to {{ .Values.server.publicUrl }} — OAuth endpoints
   are discovered automatically from /.well-known/oauth-authorization-server.
